Privacy Policy for GoBook

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Welcome to GoBook, a venue booking platform operated by Deep Hill (“GoBook”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices available to you.

By using the GoBook mobile application or website, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GoBook, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Profile photo (if provided)
  • Google account information when you sign in using Google Sign-In

Booking Information

  • Venue bookings
  • Booking history
  • Booking preferences
  • Payment status
  • Cancellation and refund details

Device Information

  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

Location Information

With your permission, GoBook may access your device location to help you discover nearby venues and improve your booking experience.

Your location is used only while you use the app and is not continuously tracked in the background.

Analytics Information

We may collect anonymous usage information including:

  • Pages visited
  • Features used
  • Session duration
  • Clicks and interactions
  • Performance metrics

This information helps us improve the quality and performance of GoBook.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Process venue bookings
  • Provide customer support
  • Send booking confirmations
  • Send cancellation updates
  • Send booking reminders
  • Process refunds
  • Improve app performance
  • Prevent fraud and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Personalize your experience

3. Notifications

GoBook may send push notifications relating to:

  • Booking confirmations
  • Booking reminders
  • Booking cancellations
  • Refund updates
  • Important account or security notifications

If you opt in, we may also send promotional offers and updates. You can disable promotional notifications at any time through your device settings.

4. Payments

Online payments are securely processed through trusted third-party payment service providers.

GoBook does not store your complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, banking passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Payment processing is governed by the privacy policies of the respective payment provider.

5. Third-Party Services

GoBook may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Google Sign-In (for authentication)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Push Notification Services
  • Payment Gateway Providers
  • Cloud Hosting Providers

These providers process information only for providing services on our behalf and are required to protect your information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only when necessary with:

  • Venue owners for processing bookings
  • Payment providers
  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Government authorities where required by applicable law

7. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted communications (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Access controls
  • Authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security monitoring

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Complete bookings
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud
  • Meet legal, accounting, taxation, or regulatory requirements

When your information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it.

9. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your GoBook account at any time.

You can:

  • Delete your account from within the GoBook application (where available), or
  • Email us at support@deephill.in requesting account deletion.

Upon verification of your request, we will permanently delete your account and associated personal information, except where retention is required by applicable law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, dispute resolution, taxation, or regulatory compliance. Google Play requires apps offering account creation to provide an accessible account deletion process both in-app and through a web resource.

10. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed and stored on secure cloud servers located in India or other countries where our trusted service providers operate.

By using GoBook, you consent to such processing in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

11. Children’s Privacy

GoBook is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will promptly delete it.

12. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Update your profile
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns

13. Compliance with Applicable Laws

We process personal information in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), where applicable.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.

Continued use of GoBook after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
