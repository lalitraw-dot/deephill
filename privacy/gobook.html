Privacy Policy for GoBook

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

GoBook (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the GoBook mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you use GoBook, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Profile information

Booking Information

  • Venue bookings
  • Booking history
  • Payment status
  • Cancellation and refund information

Device Information

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Device identifiers
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

Location Information

If you grant permission, GoBook may access your approximate or precise location to help you discover nearby venues.

Location permission is optional and can be disabled at any time from your device settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process bookings
  • Facilitate payments and refunds
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance
  • Detect fraud and misuse
  • Send booking confirmations and notifications
  • Comply with legal obligations

Payments

Online payments are processed through trusted third-party payment service providers. GoBook does not store your complete debit or credit card information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with:

  • Venue owners to manage bookings
  • Payment gateway providers
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics and crash reporting providers
  • Government or legal authorities where required by law

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Meet legal requirements
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Children’s Privacy

GoBook is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

GoBook may use third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Razorpay (where applicable)
  • PhonePe (where applicable)

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
